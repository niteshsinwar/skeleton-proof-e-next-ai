# /scorecard: judge the product before anyone else does

1. Fill in `docs/scorecard.md`: each quality of the product bar with its evidence (a link, a count or a recorded run), never an opinion.
2. Score the seven planes (experience, orchestration, intelligence, knowledge and data, platform, trust, improvement) as GOLD, SILVER or MISSING.
3. Every MISSING becomes a `docs/SCOPE.md` row or an explicit non-goal.
4. Run the cold-agent test: could a fresh session state the problem, scope, next task and stop conditions from the repo alone?
